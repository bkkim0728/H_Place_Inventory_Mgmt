Liquidity providers

Put idle assets to work in the pools traders use

Every swap pays a pool fee, and that fee goes to the liquidity providers whose range the trade crosses.

Earn fees on every trade

Fees accrue to your position in real time and compound when you collect. No lockups, no emissions schedule to track.

Concentrate your range

Choose the price band where you want to provide depth. Narrower ranges earn a larger share of fees while price stays inside them.

Withdraw any block

Positions are NFTs held in your own wallet. Remove liquidity whenever you want, with no exit fee from the protocol.

Governance

AMBR holders set fees, listings, and the treasury

Protocol parameters change only through on-chain votes. Delegate your voting power or vote on proposals directly.

How a proposal ships

  1. Forum draftDiscussion for at least 5 days.
  2. Temperature checkOff-chain signal vote, 3 days.
  3. On-chain vote7 days. Needs 40M AMBR quorum.
  4. Timelock & execute48-hour delay before contracts update.
